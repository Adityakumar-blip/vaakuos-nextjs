1. The strategy in one paragraph

The homepage targets "WhatsApp marketing + cart recovery for Shopify & WooCommerce". The blog doesn't support it yet. Build two hub posts, one for each core topic: cart recovery and Shopify/WooCommerce order operations. Surround each with specific how-to and template posts (the "spokes"). Every spoke links up to its hub, and the hub links to the matching product page (/integrations/shopify, /integrations/woocommerce, /docs/plugin/woocommerce, /calculator). This group of linked pages is what makes Google see VaakuOS as the authority on WhatsApp cart recovery for Indian D2C stores. The two series you chose, Meta Policy Watch and Alternatives/vs, capture timely searches and people close to buying.

No keyword volumes in this plan. No keyword data source (DataForSEO, Google Search Console, Ahrefs) was connected. Priorities come from live search results, competitor coverage and how closely each topic matches what you sell, not from measured search volume. Check the P1 keywords in Google Keyword Planner or GSC before writing.

  • Leave years out of new URLs. Four current URLs contain "2026", so they'll look out of date next year. Keep the old URLs, but give new posts short URLs with no year, like /blog/whatsapp-abandoned-cart-recovery.

5. Rules every post follows (from BRAND.md)

  • Every statistic links to its original source. Competitor blogs are full of unsourced figures like "60% recovery" and "98% open rate", so don't copy them.
  • Show the real message: template copy, variables, template category and send timing.
  • Give costs in INR with an "as of <Month YYYY>" date. Comparison posts carry the line "VaakuOS makes a product in this category."
  • Each post starts with a Key Takeaways box and has at least 3 internal links: one to its hub, one to a sibling post and one to a product page.
